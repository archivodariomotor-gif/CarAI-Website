Frequently asked questions

Are CarAI valuations official appraisals?

No. They are estimates based on available market data, designed so you arrive at the purchase or the negotiation informed. They don't replace a professional appraisal or a mechanical inspection.

What is a key?

A key unlocks a full analysis: fair price with market range, real comparables from listing sites, personalised negotiation arguments, a value history chart and a watermark-free PDF dossier.

Do keys expire?

No. They're yours forever. Use them whenever you need them.

What is the blurred analysis?

The analysis really runs, but the figures appear blurred: you see that your answer exists and that it's one key away. A new one recharges every 90 days.

Are there sign-up gifts?

Yes. When you create your account you get a gift valuation (the car's fair price, without the full report). And every year, on your birthday, a free key.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. For now CarAI is available on the App Store for iPhone. The Android version is coming soon.
